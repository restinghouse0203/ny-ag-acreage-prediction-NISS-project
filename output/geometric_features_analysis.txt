
    HOW THESE MODELS COMBINE GEOMETRIC FEATURES FOR CROP CLASSIFICATION:
    
    1. K-NEAREST NEIGHBORS (KNN) - SPATIAL PROXIMITY APPROACH:
    ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
    • GEOMETRIC INTEGRATION:
      - Uses Longitude_Norm & Latitude_Norm as direct spatial coordinates
      - County-level features (CNTYFIPS, County_Crop_Diversity) provide regional context
      - County_Avg_Field_Size captures local farming scale patterns
    
    • SPATIAL REASONING:
      - Calculates Euclidean distance in multi-dimensional space including coordinates
      - Nearby fields (spatially) with similar weather/history patterns are weighted heavily
      - Regional agricultural practices naturally clustered through coordinate similarity
    
    • GEOMETRIC ADVANTAGE:
      - Captures spatial autocorrelation: "neighboring fields tend to have similar crops"
      - Handles complex geographic boundaries and micro-climates
      - Good for identifying spatial clusters of specialized farming (e.g., grape regions)
    
    2. CATBOOST - HIERARCHICAL SPATIAL DECISION TREES:
    ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
    • GEOMETRIC INTEGRATION:
      - Creates decision rules combining coordinates with other features
      - Example: "If Longitude < -76.5 AND Planting_Precip > X → Corn"
      - County features enable regional specialization rules
    
    • SPATIAL REASONING:
      - Builds tree splits that naturally define geographic regions
      - Combines spatial location with weather/temporal patterns
      - Handles categorical county data effectively (built-in cat feature support)
    
    • GEOMETRIC ADVANTAGE:
      - Identifies geographic farming zones automatically through tree splits
      - Captures interaction between location and weather (altitude, lake effects)
      - Robust to outlier fields within regions
    
    MULTI-SCALE GEOMETRIC MODELING APPROACH:
    ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
    
    FINE-SCALE (Field Level):
    • Longitude_Norm, Latitude_Norm: Precise field positioning
    • Captures local variations within counties
    
    MEDIUM-SCALE (County Level):  
    • CNTYFIPS: Administrative boundaries with policy/infrastructure impacts
    • County_Crop_Diversity: Regional agricultural specialization patterns
    • County_Avg_Field_Size: Local farming scale and mechanization levels
    
    INTEGRATION BENEFITS:
    • Fields with similar coordinates + weather + history → likely similar crops
    • Regional specialization captured through county-level aggregations  
    • Multi-scale approach handles both local variations and broad regional trends
    • Geometric features provide spatial context that pure weather/history models miss
    
    EXPECTED SPATIAL PATTERNS IN NY AGRICULTURE:
    • Western NY: Corn-Soy rotations, larger fields → Longitude_Norm < -78°
    • Finger Lakes: Specialized crops, wine grapes → Central coordinates with low diversity
    • North Country: Dairy/Hay emphasis → Higher Latitude_Norm, high Hay/Grass probability
    • Hudson Valley: Diverse/mixed farming → High County_Crop_Diversity values
